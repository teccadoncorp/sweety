# Social Copy

Use this when a task asks for posts, a calendar, or launch teaser copy.

## What to produce

1. Checkout the task.
2. Read brand context and the campaign brief artifact if one exists.
3. Draft a small set, not a dump:
   - 3 Instagram captions (hook + body + CTA)
   - 3 X/Twitter posts (under 240 characters)
   - 2 LinkedIn posts (more editorial)
4. Save each platform as its own artifact, or one artifact titled "Social pack" with clear headings.
5. If the work is meant to go live, `request_approval` with `kind` = `publish`.
6. Mark the task `review` when drafts are ready for the board.

Never claim you posted to a network. Sweety does not publish in v1.
